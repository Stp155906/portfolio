# Veilbound Game Design Notes

## Core Direction

Veilbound is a mythic trial-based adventure built around sacred arenas, spirit realms, divine tests, and earned abilities. The player proves themselves through compact trials that each introduce a new mechanic and reward a permanent blessing.

## Trial 1: Canoe Trial of the Underworld

Source concept: The Trial of Mictecacihuatl.

The player boards a canoe guided through a moonlit cenote. They cannot directly steer the canoe; instead, mastery comes from shifting between realms, collecting candlelight, avoiding hostile spirits, and making a final offering.

### Mechanics

- **Realm Shift:** Toggle between the physical realm and spirit realm.
- **Candles:** Collect lights of the living to keep the route visible and power protective actions.
- **Spirit Encounters:** Spirits appear in the spirit realm and threaten the canoe.
- **Candle Drop:** Spend one candle to repel nearby spirits.
- **Final Offering:** Success grants a spirit blessing.

### Prototype Win Condition

Reach the altar with enough candlelight before spirit pressure overwhelms the canoe.

## Trial 2: Pok-A-Tok Sacred Trial

Source concept: Pok-A-Tok Sacred Trial.

The player enters a temple arena watched by divine forces. The sacred ball can be charged with spirit energy, bent by wind, blocked by spirit warriors, and fired through a high stone goal ring.

### Mechanics To Build Later

- Charge shot strength.
- Curve or wind-bend ball trajectory.
- AI spirit defenders.
- Divine wind traps.
- Win/loss cinematic consequences.

## Visual Tone

- Ancient stone, carved glyphs, marigold gold, moonlit water, jade, obsidian, and candlelight.
- The spirit realm should feel more dangerous, luminous, and unstable than the physical realm.
- UI should stay game-like and compact: status, controls, and current trial goal.

## Ability Rewards

- **Spirit Vision:** Reveal hidden paths and interactable objects.
- **Spirit Ally:** Call guidance from a friendly spirit.
- **Realm Shift:** Freely shift between realms, with stamina tradeoffs in future builds.
